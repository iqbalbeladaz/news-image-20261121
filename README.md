# News Image — 21 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261121`:
"Sum up 21 November's world news in one image" ($100 prize pool).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`).
The image is 1024×1024 PNG, well under 1 MiB. `generate.py` (Python + Pillow)
is the source that drew it; the submitted program only copies the finished image.

## Stories shown (all reported 21 November 2026)

1. **Hormuz** — Two projectile strikes on tankers near the Strait of Hormuz (UKMTO reports); oil shipments through the strait down ~15%.
2. **Ukraine** — Russian strike on Kryvyi Rih killed a paramedic and a 7-year-old boy, ~60 wounded; a Russian missile/drone incident in Poland drew NATO condemnation.
3. **Climate** — COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye slipped into overtime, nearly 200 delegations deadlocked over fossil fuels and climate finance.
4. **Markets** — Nasdaq plunged 5.3% in an AI sell-off, its worst week since 2020; the S&P 500 slipped into correction.
5. **APEC** — Xi Jinping hosted Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen; Trump touted his Putin talk on Ukraine.
6. **Gaza / Middle East** — Israeli fire killed four in Gaza; Saudi defences intercepted Houthi missiles fired at Riyadh.
7. **Peru** — Magnitude 6.7 earthquake in the central Peruvian Andes; ~170 homes affected in early reports.
8. **Trade** — The European Union and Indonesia signed their CEPA free-trade and investment agreements.

Footer: Denmark expelled Russia's defence attaché over a foiled assassination plot; a Russian cargo ship was hit by drones in the Gulf of Finland and its crew evacuated; Mexico captured FBI-wanted cartel leader "Chapo Isidro".

Sources consulted 22–23 Nov 2026 (WIB) included daily recaps and world-news
roundups for 21 Nov 2026 from Virginia Times, Gardner Magazine world briefing,
360LiveNews, Seven Lights, ChronicleAI, Standard Topic and Indonesian outlets
(InvestorTrust, Suara Muda, Garuda TV) — the stories above are the ones
corroborated across multiple of those roundups.
